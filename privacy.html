Privacy notice

What we do with personal data when your firm uses The Advice Engine, written to be read rather than skimmed.

Last updated 28 August 2026

Who we are

The Advice Engine is operated by The Advice Engine Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, company number 17404907. You can reach us at hello@theadviceengine.ai about anything in this notice.

Two different roles, and why it matters

We handle two quite different kinds of personal data, and the law treats them differently.

Your clients' data is your firm's, not ours. When an adviser puts a fact find, a valuation or a meeting transcript into the engine, your firm decides why that happens and stays the data controller. We act as your processor: we only do what your firm's use of the product asks us to do, and we do not use that material for our own purposes.

Adviser and firm account data is ours to look after. For the name and email address we use to sign you in, the record of your firm's account, and the usage records described below, we are the controller.

What we process

Sign-in details. Your name and work email address, passed to us by Microsoft when you sign in. We never see or hold your password, and we do not run our own passwords. Signing in uses your own organisation's Microsoft account and whatever multi-factor authentication your firm applies to it.

Case material you put in. The documents, notes, transcripts and text an adviser uploads, pastes or selects. This may contain personal data about your clients, including financial details. We process it for one purpose only: producing the draft documents and checks the adviser has asked for.

Usage records. Metadata about how the engine is used: who signed in, which workflow they ran, when, how long, the file names supplied, the case type and the grade an output received. These records never contain case content. We use them to run the service, spot faults, apply fair-use limits and understand which features earn their place.

Firm setup data. Your firm's name, FCA reference number, branding, fee schedule, users and their roles. This is supplied by your firm and marked as firm-supplied throughout the product.

How long case material lasts

This is the part most people want a straight answer on.

Case material is not written to disk and there is no client data store. While you are working, uploaded files are held in the server's working memory so that a document you added on one screen can be reused on another without uploading it again. That memory belongs to your browser session: it ends when the session ends, it is never visible to another user or another firm, and nothing is written to permanent storage. Closing or refreshing the browser ends the session and clears it.

Usage records and firm account data are kept for as long as your firm has an account with us, and for a reasonable period afterwards so we can answer questions about billing and past use.

Your material is not used to train AI models

The engine generates drafts using an AI model provided by Anthropic. That processing happens under commercial API terms which mean your case material is not used to train AI models, by us or by Anthropic. We do not sell data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Who else is involved

We keep the list of people who touch your data as short as we can:

  • Microsoft. Hosts the service on Azure in the UK, and provides the sign-in.
  • Anthropic. Provides the AI model that produces the drafts, under the commercial terms described above.
  • The Financial Conduct Authority's public register. When a firm signs up, we check the firm reference number it gives us against the FCA's public register API to confirm the firm exists, is currently authorised and is who it says it is.

Some of this processing takes place outside the United Kingdom. Where it does, it is covered by the contractual protections for international transfers that UK data protection law requires.

If you connect OneDrive or SharePoint

Connecting your Microsoft files is optional, and off until you choose it. When you do connect it:

  • Access is read only. The engine cannot create, change, share or delete anything.
  • The engine reads as you, so it can only ever see files you can already open yourself. It holds no access of its own and gains you no access you did not have.
  • Nothing is copied into a case except the files you tick. When the engine suggests files from a folder, it sees only file names, sizes and dates, never the contents, until you confirm.

This website

This marketing site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and contains no tracking or advertising scripts. There is nothing here to consent to. The application at app.theadviceengine.ai uses a session cookie that exists only to keep you signed in.

Keeping it secure

Access requires a Microsoft sign-in from an onboarded firm; accounts we do not recognise are refused rather than given a limited view. Data is encrypted in transit. Each firm sees only its own configuration and its own material.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, object to or restrict what we do with it, and ask for it in a portable form. Write to hello@theadviceengine.ai and we will respond within one month.

If your request is about a client's data rather than your own, the advice firm is the controller and the request should go to that firm. We will help the firm answer it.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, though we would rather you told us first so we can put it right.

Changes

If we change this notice we will change the date at the top, and we will tell firms directly about anything that materially affects them.
