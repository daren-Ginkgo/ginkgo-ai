1. Who these terms are with

These terms apply between us and the firm whose adviser or staff member uses the service ("your firm", "you"). If you are using the engine on behalf of a firm, you confirm you are authorised to accept these terms for it.

2. What the engine is, and what it is not

The Advice Engine is a document drafting and checking tool for authorised UK advice firms. It takes case material your firm supplies and produces draft documents, checks and calculations in your firm's own branding.

It is not an adviser and it is not a compliance function. Specifically, the engine:

  • does not give financial advice, to your firm or to your clients;
  • does not approve financial promotions;
  • does not certify that anything is compliant, and never marks a file compliant;
  • does not make regulated decisions of any kind.

3. Every output is a draft

This is the most important term here. Everything the engine produces is a draft until a named, qualified person at your firm has reviewed and approved it. Outputs may contain errors, omissions or misreadings of your source material, and AI-generated text can be confidently wrong.

Your firm remains responsible for the advice it gives, the documents it issues, and its own regulatory obligations. Nothing produced by the engine transfers any of that responsibility to us.

4. Who may use it

The engine is for authorised advice firms and their staff. Accounts are granted to firms we have onboarded, or to firms that pass the verification built into signup. We may decline or withdraw access where a firm does not meet those conditions.

Keep your sign-in secure, and tell us promptly if you believe an account is being misused. Sign-in uses your own organisation's Microsoft account; we never hold your password.

5. Your responsibilities

  • Review every output properly before it is used or issued. Do not pass a draft to a client unchecked.
  • Make sure you have a lawful basis for putting client personal data into the engine, and that doing so is consistent with what you have told your clients.
  • Supply accurate source material. The engine flags gaps rather than inventing facts, but it cannot detect that something you gave it is wrong.
  • Use the engine within any data restriction that applies to your account. Where your account is limited to anonymised or fictitious data, do not enter real client details.
  • Do not attempt to reverse engineer the service, resell access, or use it to build a competing product.

Where we offer a free month, no payment details are taken and the trial simply stops at the end of the month unless your firm chooses to subscribe. Trials carry a fair-use daily allowance. We may withdraw a trial where these terms are broken.

How we handle personal data, including the fact that your case material is not used to train AI models, is set out in our privacy notice, which forms part of these terms. In short: for your clients' data your firm is the controller and we act as your processor.

Using the engine gives your firm permission to use the service, not ownership of it. Your firm's branding and content remain yours, and we use them only to run the service for you.
