The loop

One firm’s file review makes every firm’s paperwork better

Because the engine is used on real cases inside network firms every week, it learns from the sharpest possible teacher: what reviewers actually push back on. When a reviewer wants something evidenced differently, that goes into the engine once — and every firm on it benefits from then on.

No generic AI vendor has that feedback loop, because no generic AI vendor files this paperwork.

Recent examples of the loop

Annual allowances table

A reviewer expected the allowances position per client in review packs. Now produced in every annual review output — evidenced from the file or flagged to confirm.

Scope-of-advice footers

Regulatory footers follow each firm’s actual permissions — a firm that does not advise on mortgages does not get a mortgage footer.

Directness over waffle

Advisers told us review reports from legacy tools bury the point. The engine’s outputs put the position and the recommendation first.

The discipline that makes it useful

The engine does not invent missing client data, does not provide financial advice, does not approve financial promotions and does not certify compliance.

Human control is part of the workflow

  • Every output is a draft until a named adviser signs it off
  • Gaps are flagged rather than quietly filled
  • Case content is not written back to your systems
  • Demonstrations and specimens are fictitious and labelled as such
