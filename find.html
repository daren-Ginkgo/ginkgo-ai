What the workflow is designed to do

Built for authorised advice firms, with the adviser responsible for review, judgement and sign-off.

✓

Verified quotes

Every surfaced quote is checked against the source document in code.

✓

Meeting transcripts first

Clients often say things in meetings that never reach the structured file, making transcripts a particularly rich source.

✓

Prioritised next conversation

The result is a tracked agenda for what to discuss next — not advice and not a review of past advice.

✓

Client facts extract

The same Find family can turn a case pack into a source-attributed update sheet, marking facts confirmed, estimated or unclear.

The discipline that makes it useful

The engine does not invent missing client data, does not provide financial advice, does not approve financial promotions and does not certify compliance.

Human control is part of the workflow

  • Every output is a draft until a named adviser signs it off
  • Gaps are flagged rather than quietly filled
  • Case content is not written back to your systems
  • Demonstrations and specimens are fictitious and labelled as such
