Every output is a draft until a named adviser signs it off.

These are not settings. They are built into every workflow and enforced in code.

How it works

Four steps, adviser in control throughout

  1. Give it the case. Upload documents, paste a transcript, or pick files straight from your firm's own client library with your own Microsoft sign-in.
  2. It drafts and data-checks. The engine reviews the material for gaps and inconsistencies first, then drafts against your firm's standards.
  3. It checks its own work. Every draft is graded against the compliance framework and revised before it reaches you, with the working shown.
  4. You review and sign off. Documents arrive as branded Word files with an adviser QA sheet. Nothing goes near a client without a named adviser's approval.
Try before you trust it: every new user gets a Test Drive - a few basic facts about a fictitious client in, a full watermarked specimen report out, through the exact live pipeline. You see precisely what to expect before any real case goes in.

Governance

Built for firms that answer to a network

  • UK-region Microsoft Azure hosting; sign-in with your firm's Microsoft accounts and MFA.
  • Named users only, per-firm isolation, unknown domains refused by default.
  • Client documents are processed in the moment, not retained; your data is not used to train models.
  • Metadata-only audit logging: who used the engine, when and for what - never case content.
  • DPIA, records of processing and data-processing agreements available for your due diligence.
