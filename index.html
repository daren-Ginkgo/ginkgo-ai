The difference

Everyone else sells you time. We can also show you revenue.

Every AI tool in this market is sold on hours saved. Useful — and hard to put in front of a board. The advice gap scanner does something different: it reads the files you already hold and finds the needs that were raised and never actioned.

Open promises. Assets mentioned once in a meeting and never traced. Exposures that only appear when two facts on the file are read together. Conflicting signals between what a client said and what the file records.

It quotes the file word for word, and every quote is verified in code against the source document — anything unverified is tagged and demoted, never quietly dropped. What comes out is a prioritised agenda for your next conversation, with a tracking sheet. It is not advice, and it is not a review of past advice.
